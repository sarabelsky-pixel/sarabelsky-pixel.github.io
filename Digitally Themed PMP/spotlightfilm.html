Role #1

Position Title: LN Concerts, Tour Marketing Coordinator

Company: Live Nation

Location: Nashville, TN

What This Role Will Do

  • Reporting to the Tour Marketer, this role will perform a range of administrative and marketing support duties for the Tour Marketing team
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams including LNE Digital and Media & Partnerships teams to maintain and track advertising budgets - including and processing incoming advertising invoices and compiling advertising settlement recap reports
  • Coordinate execution of tickets or applicable prizing for programs and promotions
  • Coordinate tour details including on sale timing, marketing instructions, etc. with internal and external stakeholders
  • Coordinate tour logistics with internal and external stakeholders
  • Liaise with internal and external teams on tour details
  • Assist with coordination, set up and on-site duties at shows, press events, etc.
  • Assist in meeting preparation with artist representatives
  • Research audience and artist demographic info to help shape marketing plans
  • Ensure all necessary show marketing information is input into Live Nation proprietary marketing tools

I would love the opportunity to be a part of the business side of concerts. Additionally, my current role as a Marketing and Communications intern at the MJ Companies has equipped me with valuable experience in content strategy, social media planning, and project management, all of which I believe are directly applicable to the demands of the Tour Marketing Coordinator role.

I have always loved music and concerts. Growing up, my family has always been very music oriented. On top of that, my uncle is Al Schnier of the band Moe., and I have been fortunate to have grown up going to his concerts and getting to go backstage. And a pretty simple one at that; to hang out and listen to their favorite band. Role #2

Position Title: Digital Media Manager

Company: AKC Museum of the Dog

Location: New York, NY

About the Job

The AKC Museum of the Dog is seeking an experienced Digital Media Manager to spearhead the Museum's daily social media, website maintenance, and marketing. This person will be tech savvy with a keen eye for detail, and the desire to innovate. As a small Museum, the ability to work collaboratively with multiple departments is essential, and self-motivation and initiative-taking are also key. This person will be given the opportunity to develop a marketing strategy on their own, with guidance from various departments and the Director. This person will also be responsible for daily social media posts, fielding responses to social media inquiries/building rapport with respondents, creating, and planning email campaigns, and keeping email lists current. This person will be required to take, edit, and upload photos and video on behalf of the Museum and create digital and (occasionally print) communications.

Production Budget

I have selected the location of Denver, CO for my location upon graduation. The cost of living here is 19% higher than it is in Indianapolis (Cost of living calculator). The $40,000 budget will be stretched there.

The first thing that I looked at when creating my budget was housing costs. I found an apartment with a monthly rent cost of $1,219.00. The apartment is a one bedroom that is 555 square feet at the Pines at 2701 Federal Blvd (Zillow). This apartment building is centrally located in Denver and is close to plenty of restaurants, shopping, entertainment, etc. I have based my Utilities cost off of the average utilities cost of a 915 square foot apartment (What is the true cost of living in Denver?). This is probably a little higher than it would actually be—simply because that is almost twice the size of the apartment that I have found. I have looked into a wifi and phone plan with Verizon and found the total cost per month to be $115/month. With all of this in mind, the total Housing costs that I found were $1,473.01.

The next piece of my budget focuses on subscriptions. I currently have my own subscriptions to Hulu, Spotify, Costco, Amazon Prime, AMC, and Apple Storage. I use my family's Apple TV, Netflix, HBO Max and every once in a while Xfinity. When I graduate and have to pay for every subscription that I use, I will likely stop using Apple TV, Netflix, and Xfinity. I have found the prices for the ad supported version of HBO and Hulu as well as AMC, Apple storage, Costco, and Amazon Prime. I am on a family plan for Spotify with my friends that has me paying $3/month. The total costs to maintain these subscriptions is $62.59/month.

The next thing that I looked at was personals costs. This includes clothes, personal care, and groceries. I based all of these numbers off of my spending habits and the cost of living in Denver, to total $430/month. I also did this for Health costs, I know I don't take any medications or anything, so my costs are relatively low, and I am not going to the doctor every month. I thought about this like a savings fund. One that collects $100/month.

For the car insurance and gas costs, I have found the average costs for Denver. For the gas costs, I calculated it based on how much I expect to drive and fill my tank every month. The total for this is $320.89/month.

The last piece was calculating miscellaneous costs including savings and this was all based off the amount of money that was remaining in my budget—$946.52.
